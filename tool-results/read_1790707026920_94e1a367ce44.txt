     1→Below is a copy-ready **master build prompt** for an AI coding agent to
     2→generate a production-ready, deployable SaaS platform for medical
     3→transcription operations. It is designed around a hybrid
     4→onsite--offshore model, AI-assisted workflow automation,
     5→human-in-the-loop clinical quality control, compliance controls, client
     6→portals, team operations, and EHR-ready integrations.
     7→
     8→**Master Build Prompt**
     9→
    10→You are a principal SaaS architect, senior full-stack engineer,
    11→healthcare workflow specialist, security engineer, DevOps engineer, and
    12→AI-agent systems designer.
    13→
    14→Build a fully functional, production-ready, deployable multi-tenant SaaS
    15→application named:
    16→
    17→MedFlow Transcribe AI
    18→
    19→Tagline: Secure AI-assisted medical transcription operations for
    20→healthcare organizations and managed offshore delivery teams.
    21→
    22→The product serves healthcare providers, hospitals, clinics,
    23→transcription service organizations, medical editors, QA teams, and
    24→onsite/offshore delivery teams.
    25→
    26→The platform must automate the complete medical transcription lifecycle:
    27→
    28→1\. Secure clinical audio/dictation intake.
    29→
    30→2\. AI speech-to-text transcription.
    31→
    32→3\. Medical-document structuring and formatting.
    33→
    34→4\. Specialty-aware terminology validation.
    35→
    36→5\. AI quality analysis and risk detection.
    37→
    38→6\. Intelligent work routing to transcriptionists, editors, QA
    39→reviewers, or escalation queues.
    40→
    41→7\. Human review and correction workflows.
    42→
    43→8\. Clarification/query workflow for unclear dictation or missing
    44→metadata.
    45→
    46→9\. Final approval and EHR-ready delivery.
    47→
    48→10\. Client reporting, operational analytics, SLA monitoring, billing,
    49→and auditability.
    50→
    51→The application must be designed for a hybrid onsite--offshore delivery
    52→model:
    53→
    54→\- Onsite/client-region account managers, implementation teams, and
    55→clinical escalation coordinators.
    56→
    57→\- Offshore transcriptionists, medical editors, QA analysts, and
    58→delivery managers.
    59→
    60→\- Healthcare client administrators, providers, and billing users.
    61→
    62→\- Super administrators operating the multi-tenant SaaS platform.
    63→
    64→Do not create a mockup, partial prototype, static dashboard, or
    65→pseudo-code-only solution. Generate a complete working application with
    66→database schema, authentication, authorization, APIs, background
    67→workers, file handling, AI workflows, dashboards, tests, documentation,
    68→Docker setup, CI/CD configuration, and deployment instructions.
    69→
    70→Use a modular monolith architecture initially, with clean module
    71→boundaries so high-volume components can later become separate services.
