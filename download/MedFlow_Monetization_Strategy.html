1. Executive Summary

MedFlow Transcribe AI is an AI-assisted medical transcription operations platform designed for white-label distribution to transcription service organizations (TSOs), healthcare BPOs, hospitals, and health systems worldwide. This document defines the complete monetization framework — pricing models, licensing terms, revenue-sharing schemes, and go-to-market positioning — optimized for international market expansion.

The global medical transcription market is valued at approximately $70 billion USD (2026 estimate) and is growing at a CAGR of 5.8% driven by the shift from on-premise dictation systems to cloud-based, AI-assisted transcription platforms. The market is highly fragmented — the top 10 TSOs hold less than 25% market share — creating a significant opportunity for a white-labeled platform that enables regional and specialty-specific BPOs to compete with modern AI capabilities without building their own technology stack.

MedFlow's monetization strategy is built on seven complementary revenue streams: (1) SaaS subscription tiers, (2) usage-based metering (audio minutes + AI processing), (3) white-label licensing fees, (4) revenue-sharing partner program, (5) professional services + implementation, (6) add-on module marketplace, and (7) API access for enterprise integrators. This multi-stream approach maximizes revenue per customer while accommodating clients of all sizes — from solo transcriptionists to multi-national health systems.

2.1 Model Selection Matrix

Partners and clients can select the model that best fits their business structure. Models are combinable — a hospital might use SaaS Subscription + Professional Services + Add-On Modules, while a BPO might use White-Label Licensing + Revenue Sharing + Usage Metering.

Subscription-First Default
Best for clinics + mid-size TSOs. Predictable monthly revenue. Includes base minutes + seats. Overage billed at usage rates.
Usage-First BPO
Best for offshore BPOs with variable volume. No minimum commitment. Pay per audio minute + per AI run. Volume discounts apply.
License-First Enterprise
Best for large BPOs + health systems. One-time license + annual maintenance. Self-hosted or dedicated cloud. Includes white-label branding.
